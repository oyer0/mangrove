UNSCII bitmap sources
=====================

Mangrove embeds the regular Unscii 8x16 bitmap by viznut. It is from the
official Unscii repository at commit
257b4dfeb50651510fe8b9cf4f151a148a4490e4
(https://github.com/viznut/unscii).

License:
Public Domain / CC0

The source bitmap is retained in `font/unscii-16.hex`. `kernel/assets/font.psf`
is generated from it by `tools/convert_unscii_hex.py`. The converter keeps
native bitmaps and does not resize glyphs.
