UNSCII bitmap sources
=====================

Mangrove embeds the regular Unscii 8x16 bitmap by viznut. The native 8x8
source is retained for converter validation; it is not embedded in Pith.
It is from the official Unscii repository at commit
257b4dfeb50651510fe8b9cf4f151a148a4490e4
(https://github.com/viznut/unscii).

License:
Public Domain / CC0

The source bitmaps are retained in `font/unscii-16.hex` and
`font/unscii-8.hex`. `kernel/assets/font.psf` is generated from the 8x16
source by `tools/convert_unscii_hex.py`. The converter keeps native bitmaps
and does not resize glyphs.
